Airport Transportation

JFK, LaGuardia, Newark & ISP Transfers

Long Island Car & Limo is the easier, cleaner, more reliable alternative to ride-share and taxis for airport runs. Whether you're heading out for a 5 AM flight or arriving on a delayed red-eye, our chauffeurs track your flight in real time, arrive 15 minutes early, and help with your luggage. Every airport transfer includes complimentary wait time, meet-and-greet at arrivals, and child seats on request.

We serve all major airports in the region — JFK (John F. Kennedy International), LGA (LaGuardia), EWR (Newark Liberty), ISP (Long Island MacArthur) and HPN (Westchester County) — with flat-rate, transparent pricing.

Airport FAQ

Airport Transfer Questions

Which airports do you serve?

We provide transfers to and from JFK, LaGuardia (LGA), Newark Liberty (EWR), Long Island MacArthur (ISP) and Westchester County (HPN), plus private terminals like Republic Airport (FRG).

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight in real time using the flight number you provide. If your arrival is delayed, your chauffeur adjusts automatically — there is no extra charge for delays caused by the airline.

How early should I book an airport transfer?

We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance, but our 24/7 dispatch can accommodate same-day and last-minute requests whenever a vehicle is available.
