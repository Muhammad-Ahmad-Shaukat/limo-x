FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide service to JFK, LaGuardia and Newark airports?

Yes. We provide 24/7 airport transportation to and from JFK, LaGuardia (LGA), Newark Liberty (EWR), and Long Island MacArthur (ISP). All pickups include flight tracking and complimentary wait time.

How do I get a price quote?

We provide custom quotes for every trip — submit the booking form or call us at (718) 618-9155 and we'll respond within minutes with a flat-rate quote tailored to your route and vehicle preference.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend reserving at least 24 hours ahead, but our 24/7 dispatch accommodates same-day and last-minute bookings whenever a vehicle is available.

Are your chauffeurs licensed and insured?

Absolutely. Every chauffeur is TLC-licensed, background-checked, and trained. Our fleet carries full commercial insurance and is inspected regularly to meet NY state regulations.
