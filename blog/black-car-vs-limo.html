Black Car vs Limo Service: Which One Should You Book?

HomeBlogBlack Car vs Limo

February 14, 2026   |   5 min read

"Black car service" and "limo service" are often used interchangeably, but they mean very different things in the chauffeured ground transportation industry. If you're trying to book the right vehicle for a wedding, an airport transfer, a business meeting or a night out, picking the wrong category can mean overpaying — or showing up in something that doesn't fit the occasion.

What is "black car service"?

Black car service is the umbrella term for chauffeured rides in standard luxury sedans and SUVs — think Cadillac XTS, BMW 7-Series, Lincoln Continental, Mercedes E/S-Class, Cadillac Escalade, Chevrolet Suburban. The car is typically painted black and operated by a TLC-licensed chauffeur. There's no "stretch" in the body — it's a normal-sized luxury vehicle.

Use a black car when:

  • You need an airport transfer (1–6 passengers)
  • You're attending a business meeting
  • You have a corporate roadshow
  • You want quiet, discreet, professional transport
  • You don't need to make a "statement" arrival

What is "limo service"?

Limo service usually refers to stretched vehicles with extended wheelbases — Lincoln MKT Stretch, Chrysler 300 Stretch, Cadillac Escalade Stretch, Hummer H2 Stretch, and so on. These vehicles have leather bench seating along the cabin, neon mood lighting, surround sound, a bar setup, and seat anywhere from 8 to 20 passengers.

Use a limo when:

  • You're celebrating a wedding, prom, sweet sixteen or anniversary
  • You have a group of 8 or more passengers
  • You want photos and an "experience" — not just transport
  • You're doing a multi-stop tour (wineries, casinos, breweries)
  • The "arrival" is part of the event

The price difference

Generally, black car service is billed per trip (flat-rate point-to-point) and is the cheaper option for 1–6 passengers. Limousines are billed hourly with a 3-hour minimum and are significantly more per hour. But for a large group, a single limo is often more cost-effective than booking 3–4 black cars to move the same number of people.

What about Sprinter vans and party buses?

If you need more capacity than a black-car SUV but don't want the "look" of a stretch, a Mercedes-Benz Sprinter van is the middle ground. Sprinters fit up to 14 passengers and are popular for wedding parties, corporate shuttles and group airport runs. Party buses are similar but lean more toward the celebration end of the spectrum.

The bottom line

If you're moving people, choose a black car. If you're celebrating, choose a limo. And if you're not sure, call us — we'll walk you through it and make sure you get the right vehicle for your day.
