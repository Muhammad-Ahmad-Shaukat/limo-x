Limo FAQ

Common Limo Questions

Is there a minimum rental time for limos?

Most limo bookings are hourly with a 3-hour minimum. For weddings and proms we offer custom packages with set start and end times — call us for a tailored quote.

Can we bring our own drinks?

Yes, passengers 21 and over may bring their own drinks. We provide complimentary water and ice; champagne service can be added on request. Open alcohol is not permitted for prom or under-21 events.

How many passengers can your largest limo hold?

Our Hummer and Escalade stretches can comfortably seat up to 18–20 passengers. For larger groups we recommend our Mercedes Sprinter party van or multi-vehicle packages.
