Chauffeur Service

Personal Chauffeurs by the Hour

Hire your own private chauffeur for the day. Our hourly chauffeur service is the perfect choice when you need a dedicated driver for multi-stop trips, shopping runs, dinner reservations, sightseeing, family outings, or back-to-back business meetings across Long Island and NYC.

Each chauffeur is TLC-licensed, uniformed, background-checked, and trained to provide quiet, discreet, white-glove service. We offer luxury sedans for solo executives, SUVs for families and small groups, and Sprinter vans for larger parties — all booked on a flexible hourly basis with a 3-hour minimum.

Chauffeur FAQ

Common Questions

What's the minimum booking time for a chauffeur?

Our hourly chauffeur service has a 3-hour minimum. You can extend the booking at any time by calling dispatch — billing continues in 30-minute increments.

Can the chauffeur wait while I shop or attend a meeting?

Absolutely. Wait time is included in your hourly booking — your driver stays with you for the entire duration.

Do you serve Manhattan, Brooklyn and Queens?

Yes — our chauffeur service covers all five boroughs of New York City, all of Long Island, and we can travel into New Jersey, Westchester, and Connecticut on request.
