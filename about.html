Who We Are

Your Local Limo & Car Service in Elmont, NY

Long Island Car & Limo is a family-owned ground transportation company headquartered at 36 Circle Dr W in Elmont, New York. For over a decade we have helped travelers, families, executives and event planners move comfortably and on time across Long Island, all five boroughs of New York City, and the surrounding tri-state area.

Our promise is simple: courteous chauffeurs, spotless vehicles, transparent pricing and dependable on-time service — 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
