Corporate Travel

Executive Transportation for Business Travelers

Long Island Car & Limo provides corporate ground transportation for law firms, financial services, tech companies, hotels, hospitals and event planners across New York City and Long Island. Our corporate programs include consolidated monthly billing, a dedicated account manager, priority dispatching, and detailed trip reporting for expense reconciliation.

Whether you need a single airport pickup for a visiting executive or a full multi-vehicle roadshow across Manhattan, Long Island and New Jersey, our team handles the logistics so your travelers can focus on business.

Corporate FAQ

Business Travel Questions

How do we set up a corporate account?

Contact us through the corporate request form or call (718) 618-9155. We'll schedule a 20-minute discovery call to understand your travel volume, billing requirements, and preferences, then set up your account within 48 hours.

Can we customize billing codes and reporting?

Yes. We support cost centers, project codes, traveler IDs, and custom report templates to match your expense systems including Concur and Expensify.

Do you handle multi-day roadshows?

Absolutely. Our roadshow team coordinates multiple vehicles, drivers, and cities, with a single dispatch contact and a real-time itinerary for all travelers.
